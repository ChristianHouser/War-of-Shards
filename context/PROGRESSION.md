# Hero Progression & Collection

## Hero Collection
- Players collect fantasy heroes.
- Heroes have fixed or semi-fixed roles that influence whether they prefer Melee or Ranged placement (exact assignment rules still open).
- Heroes can be obtained through:
  - Campaign rewards
  - Side missions (hero backstories)
  - PvP rewards
  - Gacha / recruitment system (details TBD)

## Leveling
- Heroes gain experience from battles (Campaign, Side Missions, PvP).
- Leveling increases base stats (Attack, Defense, Health, Mana regeneration rate, etc.).
- Leveling is the primary way to unlock new abilities.

## Abilities
- Every hero starts with a **Basic Attack** (low mana/stamina cost).
- As the hero levels up, they unlock additional abilities.
- Higher-tier abilities cost significantly more mana/stamina.
- Ability types can include:
  - Direct damage
  - Area damage
  - Buffs / debuffs
  - Healing
  - Positional effects (pull, push, stun, etc.)
  - Special interactions with Melee/Ranged zones

## Mana / Stamina System
- Each hero has their **own independent bar**.
- Bars fill from Match-3 matches.
- Players choose which hero’s ability or basic attack to use when they have enough resource.

## Side Missions
- Individual hero backstory missions.
- Usually smaller team sizes or special rules.
- Reward unique cosmetics, ability upgrades, or lore.
